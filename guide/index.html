Guide

This site helps you make every hero and champion in Shop Titans as strong as possible. You type in your heroes once; it compares each one against the best build it can reach and tells you, slot by slot, what to change and where to get it. It is a free guild tool, not made by or affiliated with Kabam.

Quick start (10 minutes)

  1. Sign in with Discord (top right). You can also try it without signing in; then your data stays in this browser only.
  2. Open Account and press + New hero. Copy the hero from the game: class, level, power (required), the 4 skills, and the item in each of the 6 slots with its quality, element and spirit. Every field has a short hint under it. Start with your 3–5 strongest heroes.
  3. Optional but worth it: add your storage (Epic/Legendary items and enchants, Titan Souls, skill dice) and your blueprints. Without them the site still works, but it cannot tell you "use the one in your storage" or "craft it yourself".
  4. Press Compute. It takes about 10–60 seconds per hero, depending on your device. Keep the tab open and in front (browsers slow down background tabs).
  5. Open Heroes, pick a hero, and scroll to Review / what-if. Then look at the Bucket list for your shopping list.

After you change something in the game (new item, enchant, level), update the hero in Account and press Compute again.

The pages

Account
Where your data lives: heroes, storage, blueprints. Import / Export make a backup file or move data between browsers. Compute runs the review for all heroes.
Heroes
One hero at a time (pick it at the top). You see the class, skills with their tiers, the stats as the game shows them next to the site's own calculation ("calc"), the element bar, and the 6 slots. Every stat opens a popup with the maths behind it. At the bottom, Review / what-if shows how far the hero is from their best build and what to change.
Team
Your whole roster in charts: power now and after the plan, gear quality per slot, missing enchants, class and element coverage, and skill ratings. Every chart has a one-line takeaway and a table with the numbers.
Bucket list
Everything you need to gather for the plan, grouped by upgrade (one hero slot, or one promotion), with needed / you have / missing, biggest win first. You can type how many you already have; the list keeps your numbers.
Names everywhere
Any item, spirit, element or skill name on any page is a link: tap it for what it does, its stats per quality and how to get it.

Reading a hero's review

"82.7% of the best possible → 99.5% with the plan"
The first number is the hero's power now compared with their ceiling: the best build they can wear (any item in Epic, plus your own Legendaries, with the best enchants), keeping today's skills. The second is where the hero ends up if you follow the plan.
Big gaps
The few things that matter most for this hero: a promotion that is waiting, an element too low for the next skill tier, a weak skill worth rerolling, items below Epic, or one slot that is far behind.
Per slot
What the slot wears now and the recommended change: item, quality, element and spirit, the power gain (measured by the calculator, not guessed), and the source: Storage already yours, free · Craft you have the blueprint · Market buy it from other players · Rare Antique Store, packs or events, only if you happen to have it. Sometimes a Market or Legendary option is listed on the side when it is clearly better.
What-if
Try any item, quality, element or spirit in any slot and see the new stats and power before you spend anything in the game.
Confidence
How close the calculator's power is to the power you typed. High (within 1%): the numbers are solid. Medium (within 5%): the order of moves is right, exact gains may be a little off. Low: check what you typed (a wrong quality or a missing enchant is the usual cause); the ranking still helps.
Skills
Each skill has a community rating for its class (S best, then A, B, C, D). B and below are reroll candidates; the review shows the chance a reroll gives something better or worse.

How it chooses

  • Epic is the default. Lower qualities are never suggested. Legendary only where the gain is big, because Legendaries are hard to get.
  • What you own comes first (free), then craft (cheaper than buying), then the Market, then rare sources.
  • Each stored item and enchant goes to one hero only, the one it helps most, so two heroes never count on the same piece.
  • Luck is marked as luck. Skill rerolls (dice) and Legendary fusion can end worse; the site shows the odds and never treats them as sure.
  • Gems are never part of the plan.
  • Every gain is calculated, using the game's formulas and data, and checked against the power you typed.

Glossary

Power
The hero's power number in the game. The site needs it to check its own maths.
calc
The site's calculator: it rebuilds the hero's stats and power from class, level, items, enchants, skills and card level.
Ceiling
The strongest build the hero can wear today (see "Reading a hero's review").
Plan
The set of changes the review recommends across all heroes, with each stored piece given to one hero.
Element
The hero's element number. Skills reach higher tiers (T2–T4) when the element passes thresholds; the Heroes page shows the bar and the thresholds.
Element / spirit enchant
The two enchant boxes on every item. Elements add stats and element points; spirits add effects (e.g. +8% Attack).
Affinity
Some items have an element or spirit they "like"; that enchant gives a bigger bonus on that item.
Promotion · Titan Soul
Promoting a hero (e.g. Ninja → Sensei) needs a Titan Soul and raises their stats and skill tiers. The review measures the gain.
Reroll · skill dice
Changing one skill at random with a Basic, Super, Mega or Perfect Skill Die. The reroll planner says which skill to roll and when to stop.
Seeds
Permanent stat boosts (Vitality, Power, Resistance). The game does not show how many a hero has, so the site estimates it.
Card level
The class card in Collection Book → Card Collection; it raises the hero's stats a little.
Needed / Have / Missing
Bucket list columns: how many the plan needs, how many you own, how many you still have to craft or buy.
STC BiS
The community's "best in slot" set for the class, shown for comparison.

Your data

  • Signed in with Discord: your heroes, storage and blueprints are stored in a database row that only your account can read or write. The site only gets your Discord name, avatar and e-mail.
  • Not signed in: everything stays in this browser. Clearing the browser's data deletes it, so use Export for a backup.
  • The calculations run in your own browser. Nothing about your heroes is sent anywhere else.
  • Results (reviews, plan, bucket list) are kept on the device you computed them on; on a new device press Compute once.

FAQ

Why is Compute slow?
For every hero it tries every useful item, quality and enchant in every slot, and calculates the real power of each. That runs on your device: about 10–60 seconds per hero. A computer is faster than a phone, and a tab in front is faster than one in the background.
The calc power differs from the game.
Check the qualities (Epic vs Legendary), the enchants and the card level you typed. Seeds the game does not show can also explain a small gap.
I can't find an item in the list.
The lists only show items that fit that slot for that class, from tier 8 up. Newer items appear after the game data is updated.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. The first load is heavier (about 15 MB); after that it is quicker.
Some text mentions "the phone" or "a session".
The tool started as one player's helper that reads the game from their phone. For you, "the phone" means what you typed in Account.
